# Revenue Recognition Policy

Acme Consulting LLC recognizes revenue in accordance with ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers). This policy applies to all revenue streams recorded in the general ledger.

## Consulting Revenue (Account 4000)

Consulting revenue is recognized over the period of service delivery using the input method (hours incurred as a percentage of estimated total hours).

### Fixed-Fee Engagements

- Revenue is recognized proportionally as services are performed
- Progress is measured by hours incurred relative to total estimated hours
- If estimated hours change, the percentage of completion is revised prospectively
- Losses on contracts are recognized immediately when identified

### Time-and-Materials Engagements

- Revenue is recognized as hours are incurred at the contracted billing rate
- Revenue equals billable hours multiplied by the agreed hourly rate
- No estimation of completion is required
- Unbilled hours at month-end are accrued to Accounts Receivable (Account 1100)

### Retainer Arrangements

- Monthly retainers are recognized ratably over the service period
- Unused retainer hours do not roll over unless the contract explicitly allows it
- Amounts received in advance of service delivery are recorded as Deferred Revenue (Account 2200)
- If a client prepays for 3 months, each month recognizes 1/3 of the total

## Training Revenue (Account 4100)

Training revenue is recognized at the point in time when the training session is delivered.

### Multi-Day Training Programs

- Revenue for multi-day programs is recognized on a per-day basis as each session is completed
- Materials provided in advance do not trigger recognition until the training session occurs
- Cancellation fees are recognized when the cancellation occurs and are non-refundable

### Self-Paced Training

- Revenue for self-paced training packages is recognized upon delivery of access credentials to the client
- No ongoing performance obligation exists after access is granted

## Revenue Cut-Off Procedures

### Month-End

- All consulting hours through the last business day of the month must be entered by close of business on the 2nd business day of the following month
- The controller reviews unbilled hours and records revenue accruals for services performed but not yet invoiced
- Deferred Revenue (Account 2200) is reviewed and revenue is released for completed performance obligations

### Quarter-End

- Extended review of all open contracts for changes in estimated hours (fixed-fee) or scope changes
- Reconciliation of Deferred Revenue to supporting contract schedules
- Documentation of significant judgments (e.g., percentage of completion estimates)
