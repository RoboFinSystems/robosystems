# Month-End Close Checklist

This checklist defines the standard month-end close procedures for Acme Consulting LLC. Target close completion is 5 business days after month-end. All tasks are performed by the controller unless otherwise noted.

## Day 1 — Cut-Off and Data Collection

### Revenue Cut-Off

- [ ] Verify all consulting hours through month-end are entered in the time tracking system
- [ ] Review unbilled hours report and accrue revenue for services performed but not invoiced
- [ ] Release Deferred Revenue (Account 2200) for completed performance obligations
- [ ] Confirm Training Revenue (Account 4100) entries match completed sessions

### Expense Cut-Off

- [ ] Collect all outstanding vendor invoices received through the close date
- [ ] Review corporate credit card transactions and ensure all charges are posted
- [ ] Record Accounts Payable (Account 2000) for invoices received but not yet entered
- [ ] Accrue known expenses without invoices to Accrued Expenses (Account 2100)

## Day 2 — Payroll and Fixed Entries

### Payroll Processing

- [ ] Verify payroll was processed for the month (Salaries — Account 5000)
- [ ] Confirm employer tax accruals are recorded in Accrued Expenses (Account 2100)
- [ ] Review and approve any bonus or commission accruals

### Fixed Recurring Entries

- [ ] Post monthly rent entry: Debit Rent (Account 5100), Credit Cash (Account 1000)
- [ ] Post insurance amortization: Debit Insurance (Account 5300), Credit Prepaid Expenses (Account 1200)
- [ ] Post depreciation: Debit Expenses, Credit Accumulated Depreciation (Account 1600)
- [ ] Review loan payment allocation: principal to Loan Payable (Account 2500), interest to expense

## Day 3 — Reconciliations

### Bank Reconciliation

- [ ] Obtain bank statement for all accounts
- [ ] Reconcile Cash (Account 1000) to bank statement balance
- [ ] Identify and investigate outstanding checks older than 90 days
- [ ] Identify and record any bank fees or interest not yet posted
- [ ] Document reconciling items with expected clearing dates

### Accounts Receivable Reconciliation

- [ ] Run AR aging report from the billing system
- [ ] Reconcile Accounts Receivable (Account 1100) to the aging report total
- [ ] Review invoices outstanding > 60 days and escalate collection
- [ ] Assess need for bad debt reserve (currently no allowance account — revisit if AR > 90 days grows)

### Accounts Payable Reconciliation

- [ ] Run AP aging report
- [ ] Reconcile Accounts Payable (Account 2000) to the aging report total
- [ ] Confirm no duplicate payments exist

## Day 4 — Review and Adjustments

### Trial Balance Review

- [ ] Generate trial balance and verify debits equal credits
- [ ] Compare each account balance to prior month — investigate variances > 10%
- [ ] Review Retained Earnings (Account 3100) — should only change from net income close
- [ ] Verify Common Stock (Account 3000) is unchanged (no equity events this month)

### Adjusting Entries

- [ ] Post any correcting entries identified during reconciliations
- [ ] Review prepaid schedules and confirm amortization is current
- [ ] Ensure all intercompany transactions are eliminated (if applicable)
- [ ] Document the purpose of every adjusting entry

## Day 5 — Reporting and Sign-Off

### Financial Statements

- [ ] Prepare income statement (Revenue accounts 4000-4100, Expense accounts 5000-5600)
- [ ] Prepare balance sheet (Asset accounts 1000-1600, Liability accounts 2000-2500, Equity accounts 3000-3100)
- [ ] Calculate key metrics: gross margin, operating margin, cash runway
- [ ] Compare actuals to budget and document significant variances

### Management Review

- [ ] Controller reviews and approves all reconciliations
- [ ] Prepare close summary memo highlighting unusual items
- [ ] Archive supporting documentation in the close binder
- [ ] Mark the period as closed — no further entries without controller approval

## Emergency Close Adjustments

If a material adjustment is discovered after close:

1. Controller must approve the re-opening of the period
2. Post the adjustment with a memo explaining the reason
3. Re-run financial statements
4. Document the late adjustment in the next month's close summary
