# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

This policy governs the classification, approval, and recording of all operating expenses for Acme Consulting LLC.

## Expense Account Structure

| Account | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5000 | Salaries | Employee compensation, bonuses, commissions |
| 5100 | Rent | Office lease payments |
| 5200 | Utilities | Electric, internet, phone, water |
| 5300 | Insurance | General liability, professional liability, property |
| 5400 | Marketing | Advertising, events, sponsorships, content |
| 5500 | Office Supplies | Consumables, small equipment under capitalization threshold |
| 5600 | Professional Fees | Legal, audit, tax preparation, outside consultants |

## Approval Thresholds

| Amount | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Department manager |
| $500 — $2,500 | Director |
| $2,500 — $10,000 | Controller |
| Over $10,000 | CEO + Controller |

All recurring commitments (subscriptions, leases, retainers) require controller approval regardless of amount.

## Capitalization Policy

### Capitalization Threshold

Expenditures of $2,500 or more with a useful life exceeding one year are capitalized to Equipment (Account 1500). Items below this threshold are expensed to Office Supplies (Account 5500).

### Depreciation Method

- Method: Straight-line
- Computer equipment: 3 years
- Office furniture: 7 years
- Leasehold improvements: Lesser of useful life or remaining lease term
- Depreciation is recorded monthly to Accumulated Depreciation (Account 1600)

### Examples

| Item | Amount | Treatment | Account |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop | $1,800 | Expense | Office Supplies (5500) |
| Server | $5,000 | Capitalize | Equipment (1500), depreciate over 3 years |
| Desk | $3,200 | Capitalize | Equipment (1500), depreciate over 7 years |
| Software subscription | $6,000/yr | Expense monthly | Professional Fees (5600) — $500/month |

## Prepaid Expenses

### When to Prepay

Payments covering future periods longer than one month are recorded as Prepaid Expenses (Account 1200) and amortized monthly over the coverage period.

### Common Prepaids

- Annual insurance premiums: Debit Prepaid Expenses (1200), amortize monthly to Insurance (5300)
- Annual software licenses paid upfront: Debit Prepaid Expenses (1200), amortize monthly to Professional Fees (5600)
- Prepaid rent (e.g., last month's rent deposit): Remains in Prepaid Expenses (1200) until applied

### Amortization Schedule

The controller maintains a prepaid amortization schedule listing:

- Vendor name and description
- Total prepaid amount
- Coverage period (start and end dates)
- Monthly amortization amount
- Remaining balance

This schedule is reviewed monthly during close (Day 2 of the close checklist).

## Employee Reimbursements

### Eligible Expenses

- Client-related travel (airfare, hotel, meals, ground transportation)
- Business meals with clients or prospects (document attendees and business purpose)
- Professional development (conferences, courses, certifications)
- Home office supplies for remote employees (pre-approved list)

### Submission Requirements

- Receipts required for all expenses over $25
- Expense reports due within 30 days of the expense
- Reports submitted after 60 days require director approval and may be denied
- Mileage reimbursement uses the current IRS standard rate

### Recording

Employee reimbursements are recorded to the appropriate expense account based on nature:

- Travel meals → Marketing (5400)
- Client entertainment → Marketing (5400)
- Office supplies → Office Supplies (5500)
- Professional development → Professional Fees (5600)

Reimbursements pending payment are recorded as Accrued Expenses (Account 2100) until paid.

## Vendor Payments

### Payment Terms

- Standard payment terms: Net 30
- Early payment discounts: Take if discount exceeds 1% (record as reduction to expense)
- Recurring vendor payments: Set up auto-pay where possible, review quarterly

### Recording

- Invoice received: Debit appropriate expense account, Credit Accounts Payable (2000)
- Payment made: Debit Accounts Payable (2000), Credit Cash (1000)
