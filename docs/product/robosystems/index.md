RoboSystems gives AI clients a financial knowledge graph to work on: your company's books and reports, your portfolios, or the SEC filings of public companies. You connect Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client, sign in, choose a graph, and ask in plain language. These guides cover what's the same whichever graph you use.

## Start here

1. [Connect Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client](connect-an-mcp-client.md): one address, a sign-in, and the setup for each client.
2. [Sign-in and graph access](oauth-and-graph-scope.md): the consent screen, one connection per graph, switching graphs, and revoking access.

## Working with graphs

- [Analyze SEC filings with Claude or ChatGPT](sec-filings.md): statements, comparisons, disclosures and filing text for 8,000+ public companies.
- [Graphs, tiers and credits](graphs-tiers-and-credits.md): what a graph is, how tiers differ, and what uses credits.
