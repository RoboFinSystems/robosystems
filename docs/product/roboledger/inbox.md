**Ledger → Inbox** holds events that have been captured and have not posted to the ledger yet. Open one, preview the journal entry, and approve it so it posts, or reject it.

Transactions that already live in QuickBooks as journal entries usually go straight to the journal on sync. The Inbox is for work that still needs a person: a captured event you have not approved, a bank line that needs an account, a manual event you entered to review first.

Nothing in the Inbox posts until you approve it. Approving writes to the RoboLedger ledger. It does not write to QuickBooks until you post an entry, which for most people is the close. See [Nothing writes to QuickBooks until you post](quickbooks-write-back.md).

## Open an event

Each row is one event: its type (invoice, bill, payment, bank line), the counterparty if there is one, the amount and the status.

- **Captured** — in the Inbox, not posted.
- **Classified** — an account (or split) has been chosen; still not posted.
- **Committed** — posted to the ledger as a draft. The close posts drafts.
- **Voided** — rejected.

Open a row to see the event, preview the debits and credits, and approve or reject.

## Bank-feed lines

A line from a connected bank feed arrives captured, often with a suggested account. It does not post until you pick the other side of the entry (or accept the suggestion) and approve it. An internal transfer between two known bank accounts is already classified and can be approved as it is.

An unclassified bank line is refused at approve. Choose an account first.

## Filter and follow a counterparty

Filter by type, status, source or the person or company the event is with. From **Agents**, you can open a customer or vendor and jump to their events in the Inbox.

## Try asking

- "What is sitting in the Inbox that hasn't posted?"
- "Show me captured bank lines and suggest an account for each."
- "Approve the ones I've classified, and leave the rest."
