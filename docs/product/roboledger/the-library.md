**Library** is the reporting vocabulary your chart of accounts maps into. Each concept has a name, a definition and a place in the statements. When you are deciding where an account belongs, look here first, then apply the mapping in **Ledger → Chart of Accounts**. See [Map your chart of accounts](map-your-chart-of-accounts.md).

## What you will see

The library lists the taxonomies on this graph: the standard US GAAP reporting concepts, the statement structures they roll up into, your own chart of accounts, and any reporting extensions this company has added.

Open a concept to read its definition, whether it is a balance or a flow, and which statement line it belongs to. Open a structure to see how those concepts nest: cash under current assets, current assets under assets, and so on.

This is the same vocabulary public companies report in, which is why a mapped chart of accounts can be compared with SEC filers. See [Compare with public companies](compare-with-public-companies.md).

## When to use it

- An account is unmapped and you want the concept, not just a guess from the name.
- A match is flagged and you want to read what the concept actually means.
- You are looking at an "other" mapping and want a more specific concept.

Your AI assistant can list unmapped accounts and suggest a concept for each one. Its suggestions use no credits. Confirm them against the library when the name is close and the meaning might not be.

## Try asking

- "Open the library concept for deferred revenue and tell me which statement line it rolls up to."
- "Which concepts sit under operating expenses?"
- "We added a contractor-costs account. Which library concept should it map to?"
