**Library** is the reporting vocabulary your chart of accounts maps into. Each concept has a name, a definition and a place on the statements. When you are deciding where an account belongs, look here first, then apply the mapping in **Ledger → Chart of Accounts**. See [Map your chart of accounts](map-your-chart-of-accounts.md).

## What you will see

Pick a taxonomy at the top. **rs-gaap** is the library's reporting concepts, drawn from the US GAAP taxonomy, and the one you will use most. **fac** is a smaller set of fundamental accounting concepts. Your own chart of accounts and any reporting extensions this company has added are listed too, each with how many elements it holds.

**Browse** lists the concepts. Open one to read its definition, whether it is a debit or a credit, and whether it is a balance at a point in time or a flow over a period.

**Hierarchy** shows where concepts sit on each statement. Choose **Presentation** for the statement's layout or **Calculation** for how its lines add up, then pick the statement: cash under current assets, current assets under assets, and so on.

The concepts are drawn from the US GAAP taxonomy that public companies report in, which is why a mapped chart of accounts can be compared with SEC filers. See [Compare with public companies](compare-with-public-companies.md).

## When to use it

- An account is unmapped and you want the concept, not just a guess from the name.
- A match is flagged and you want to read what the concept actually means.
- You are looking at an "other" mapping and want a more specific concept.

Your AI assistant can list unmapped accounts and suggest a concept for each one. Its suggestions use no credits. Confirm them against the library when the name is close and the meaning might not be.

## Try asking

- "Look up the library concept for deferred revenue. Is it a balance or a flow, and where does it sit on the balance sheet?"
- "Which concepts sit under operating expenses?"
- "We added a contractor-costs account. Which library concept should it map to?"
